FAQs

Questions we hear often

Which services does Optipay offer?

We provide debt collection, skip tracing, call centre outsourcing, HR outsourcing, payroll administration, reporting and analytics, and back-office support, together or as stand-alone services.

How quickly can we get started?

After a discovery call and agreement on scope, most campaigns go live within two to three weeks, depending on team size and integration needs.

How do you handle customer data and compliance?

We follow strict confidentiality and data-protection practices, monitor calls for quality, and operate with respectful, ethical engagement standards.

How is pricing structured?

Pricing depends on the service and volume. Collections can be performance-based, while call centre services use agreed monthly models. Contact us for a tailored quote.

Can we scale the team up or down?

Yes. Our teams flex with seasonal demand, so you only commit to the capacity you need.
